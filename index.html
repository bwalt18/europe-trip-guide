Rome

12–16 Oct · 4 nights

Mon–Fri stay = thinner crowds. Nearly everything is walkable; metro single €1.50 (poss. €2). City tax €4–6/person/night, usually cash.

Back to Rome & home

29/30 Oct – 1 Nov
⚠ Naples airport is CLOSED all of November 2026 Runway works. You fly from Rome FCO so you're fine — but there's no Naples backup, so lock the Rome return in early.
Two good ways back — book now

✈ FLY (recommended) — 30 Oct €55–90 all-inTrain Cefalù→Palermo €6.80 → Prestia e Comande bus to airport ~€6.50 → Palermo→Rome FCO: ~18 flights/day (Ryanair/Wizz from €25–60, ITA €80–130), 1h10. In Rome by lunch.

🌙 SLEEPER — 29 Oct €45–90Intercity Notte calls Cefalù ~21:23, crosses the strait on the ferry while you sleep, Roma Termini ~10:30. Saves a hotel night — book on Trenitalia now.

Either way: 31 Oct = full free day in Rome — last carbonara, Trastevere wander, anything you missed.

1 Nov — departure day notes
  • All Saints' Day (Sunday): Vatican CLOSED; state museums (Colosseum etc.) are FREE that morning if you have spare hours — crowded though.
  • Leonardo Express to FCO €14, every 15 min — allow 3 h before an international flight.
  • Taxi flat €55 (white "Roma Capitale" cars only).

Money

  • Best setup: Up Bank debit (0% FX, unlimited free o/s ATM) as primary + Wise as backup. Order both NOW (delivery 1–2 weeks).
  • ATMs: use bank ATMs (Intesa Sanpaolo, UniCredit, BNL). Never Euronet. Always choose "withdraw in EUR" — the "convert to AUD" offer costs 5–10%.
  • Cash: keep €200–300. Needed for: SITA buses, tabacchi, market stalls, small trattorias, city tax at hotels.
  • Tipping: not expected. The €1–3 "coperto" on the bill is normal, not a scam. Round up or leave €2–5 for great service.
  • Food budget: ~€26/day cheap (bar breakfast + street lunch + pizza) · €42–57/day with trattoria dinners. Lunch menù del giorno = 30–40% cheaper than dinner.

Phone & data

  • Get: Airalo "Eurolink" eSIM, 50GB / 90 days ≈ A$148 — one plan for the whole trip. Install via the app before you fly. (Cheaper: Nomad 2×50GB/30d ≈ A$92, needs a mid-trip switch.)
  • Don't use Aussie roaming — $5–10/day = A$225–450 for the trip.
  • Before departure, on wifi: download offline Google Maps (Rome, Naples/Amalfi, Calabria, Sicily) + Google Translate Italian pack.
  • Install: Trenitalia · Italo · Ferryhopper · Moovit (Rome transit) · Unico Campania (Naples-area tickets) · FreeNow (taxis) · TheFork (restaurant deals) · XE (currency).
  • Power: Australian Type I plugs don't fit — bring 2× Type C ("Europlug") adapters. Same voltage, no converter needed.

Safety & scams

These are safe places — the risk is petty theft, not violence. The whole game is bags-in-front in crowds. Official advice: Smartraveller — Italy.

  • Pickpocket zones: Termini station, Metro Line A, bus 64, Trevi crowds; Naples Garibaldi station and the Circumvesuviana train — zip everything, bag on your front.
  • Scams: anyone tying a "free" bracelet on you or holding a petition clipboard — hard no, keep walking. Taxi: only white official cars, meter on Tariff 1, or the FreeNow app.
  • Validate paper train/bus tickets in the little machines before boarding — €100 fine.
  • Carry your passport on Pompeii day (named tickets); otherwise a photo/copy + passport in the room safe.

Packing

  • Bag: 40–50L backpack or small soft case — NOT a big roller. Positano/Capri/train stations = endless stairs; ferries charge for big bags.
  • 1 week of clothes, laundry mid-trip (lavanderia wash+dry ~€14–20, or hotel service).
  • Layers: t-shirts + light jumper + compact rain jacket; one warmer layer for late-Oct evenings (12–15°C).
  • Real walking shoes (Pompeii stones; La Rocca requires closed shoes) + swimmers + quick-dry towel.
  • Hat, sunscreen, compact umbrella, scarf (doubles as church shoulder-cover), 2× Type C power adapters, refillable bottle (Rome's 2,500 nasoni fountains are free & safe).
