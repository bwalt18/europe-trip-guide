What to book & when

🔴 Book NOW (late August)
  • All hotels — especially Positano & Capri (few budget rooms, some close for winter — confirm they're open your dates) and the 31 Oct Rome night (All Saints' weekend spike).
  • Vatican Museums — museivaticani.va, €25 A$43. 60-day window for your dates is already open. Closed Sundays.
  • Borghese Gallery — tosc.it, €18. Timed slots, no walk-ups, closed Mondays.
  • Rome→Naples train 16 Oct — trenitalia.com / italotreno.com. Super Economy from €9–19 now; walk-up is €40–55.
  • Naples→Lamezia Terme train 22 Oct — advance fares €20–45.
  • Palermo→Rome flight 30 Oct — Ryanair/Wizz/ITA, ~18/day, from €25–60 now. (Or the Cefalù 21:23 sleeper train, €45–90.)
  • Travel insurance — comprehensive 7-week Europe cover ≈ A$350–700 (Fast Cover, Cover-More…). Medicare deal with Italy does NOT cover medicines/tests.
  • eSIM — Airalo "Eurolink" 50GB/90-days ≈ A$148, one plan for Greece+Italy. Install before flying.
  • Cards — order Up Bank (free o/s ATMs) + Wise as backup. Takes 1–2 weeks.
🟠 September
  • Colosseum — set an alarm: 12 Sept, 9:00am Italy (5pm AEST). Tickets release exactly 30 days ahead on ticketing.colosseo.it, €18 (Full Experience €24). Morning slots go within hours.
  • Pompeii — pompeiisites.org → Vivaticket, €20. Named ticket — must match your passport. Book by late Sept for a morning slot on 17 Oct.
🟡 1–2 weeks before each leg

Rome

12–16 Oct · 4 nights

Mon–Fri stay = thinner crowds. Nearly everything is walkable; metro single €1.50 (poss. €2). City tax €4–6/person/night, usually cash.

Money

  • Best setup: Up Bank debit (0% FX, unlimited free o/s ATM) as primary + Wise as backup. Order both NOW (delivery 1–2 weeks).
  • ATMs: use bank ATMs (Intesa Sanpaolo, UniCredit, BNL / Alpha, NBG in Greece). Never Euronet. Always choose "withdraw in EUR" — the "convert to AUD" offer costs 5–10%.
  • Cash: keep €200–300. Needed for: SITA buses, tabacchi, market stalls, small trattorias, city tax at hotels.
  • Tipping: not expected. The €1–3 "coperto" on the bill is normal, not a scam. Round up or leave €2–5 for great service.
  • Food budget: ~€26/day cheap (bar breakfast + street lunch + pizza) · €42–57/day with trattoria dinners. Lunch menù del giorno = 30–40% cheaper than dinner.

Phone & data

  • Get: Airalo "Eurolink" eSIM, 50GB / 90 days ≈ A$148 — ONE plan covers Greece + Italy the whole trip. Install via the app before you fly. (Cheaper: Nomad 2×50GB/30d ≈ A$92, needs a mid-trip switch.)
  • Don't use Aussie roaming — $5–10/day = A$225–450 for the trip.
  • Before departure, on wifi: download offline Google Maps (Rome, Naples/Amalfi, Calabria, Sicily) + Google Translate Italian pack.
  • Install: Trenitalia · Italo · Ferryhopper · Moovit (Rome transit) · Unico Campania (Naples area tickets) · FreeNow (taxis) · TheFork (restaurant deals) · XE (currency).
  • Power: Australia Type I plugs don't fit — bring 2× Type C ("Europlug") adapters. Voltage is the same, no converter needed.

Safety & scams

These are safe places — the risk is petty theft, not violence. The whole game is bags-in-front in crowds.

  • Pickpocket zones: Termini station, Metro Line A, bus 64, Trevi crowds; Naples Garibaldi station and the Circumvesuviana train (worst line in the country — zip everything, bag on your front).
  • Scams: anyone tying a "free" bracelet on you or holding a petition clipboard — hard no, keep walking. Taxi: only white official cars, meter on Tariff 1, or FreeNow app.
  • Validate paper train/bus tickets in the little machines before boarding — €100 fine.
  • Carry your passport on Pompeii day (named tickets); otherwise a photo/copy + passport in the room safe.

Packing

  • Bag: 40–50L backpack or small soft case — NOT a big roller. Positano/Capri/train stations = endless stairs; ferries charge for big bags.
  • 1 week of clothes, laundry mid-trip (lavanderia wash+dry ~€14–20, or hotel service).
  • Layers: t-shirts + light jumper + compact rain jacket; one warmer layer for late-Oct evenings (12–15°C).
  • Real walking shoes (Pompeii stones, La Rocca requires closed shoes) + swimmers + quick-dry towel.
  • Hat, sunscreen, compact umbrella, scarf (doubles as church shoulder-cover), 2× Type C power adapters, refillable bottle (Rome's 2,500 nasoni fountains are free & safe).
